What is Testosterone Replacement Therapy?

TRT is treatment intended to restore testosterone in men with clinically low levels. A provider evaluates symptoms, lab results, medical history, and treatment risks before deciding whether it is appropriate.

Will TRT improve energy, mood, or libido?

Some people with low testosterone may experience improvement in certain symptoms with treatment, but results vary. A provider can discuss realistic expectations based on your health and evaluation.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Testosterone is a prescription medication. A licensed provider must determine whether treatment is appropriate before it can be dispensed.

Is TRT right for everyone?

No. TRT has potential risks and may not be appropriate for some people. Your provider will review your health history, discuss risks and benefits, and recommend monitoring if treatment is prescribed.
