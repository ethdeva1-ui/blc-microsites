Common questions

What is Trimix?

Trimix is a compounded injectable combining three medications (papaverine, phentolamine and alprostadil) used to treat erectile dysfunction.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. A licensed provider must review your information and approve treatment before anything is dispensed.

Who should not use Trimix?

Trimix is not right for everyone. Your provider will review your history, current medications and conditions to determine whether it is safe for you.

Is Trimix FDA-approved?

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a prescription.
